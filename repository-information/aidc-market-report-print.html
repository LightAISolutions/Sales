Turbines: the slot is the product

  • GE Vernova holds 116 GW of committed gas capacity — 53 GW of equipment backlog plus 63 GW of deposit-backed slot reservations now selling against 2031 delivery (Turbomachinery Magazine, 2026-07 — GE Vernova dossier). Reservations price 10–20 points above the existing backlog, taken with ≈20% cash down that converts to orders — the clearest scarcity-rent evidence in the sector (December 2025 Investor Update transcript, 2025-12-09). Output ramps ≈20 GW/yr → 24 GW (2028) → actions toward 30 GW (2030), and GE Vernova expects to be “largely sold out of 2030 deliveries by end of 2026” (Utility Dive, 2025-12).
  • Siemens Energy: large-frame slots nearly full through 2028, and slot-reservation fees are back for the first time since the early-2000s gas boom (POWER Magazine, 2025-02 — Siemens Energy dossier). Unit sales nearly doubled in one year — 194 turbines in FY25 against 100 in FY24 (Siemens Energy release, 2025-11-14).
  • The demand shock is documented from the buyer side too: xAI signed ≈$3.7B of turbine commitments in four months of 2026 — $805M plus $925M purchase agreements through 2029 plus a ≈$2.0B mobile-fleet acquisition (SpaceX Form S-1, 2026-05-20 — xAI dossier). Analysis That makes it one of the largest mobile-turbine buyers anywhere, in a market where every AIDC developer is chasing the same units. Utilities are in the same queue: Xcel ordered 10 × SGT6-5000F (2,088 MW) for Texas — “dispatchable power is no longer optional” (Siemens Energy release, 2025-10-01).
  • Analysis — High GE Vernova's pricing power is real but it is scarcity rent, not durable moat: reservations imply ≈$3,000/kW combined-cycle pricing, roughly 3x in three years, while Siemens Energy doubled unit sales and Mitsubishi calls its own +30% capacity add “not enough” — competitor supply lands in the same 2028–2030 windows GE Vernova is now selling (GE Vernova dossier).

  • Citations. (Source label, date — Company dossier). The label matches the dossier's source entry, whose URL field holds the link; dates are source publication dates — YYYY-MM when only the month is known, absent for undated evergreen pages.
