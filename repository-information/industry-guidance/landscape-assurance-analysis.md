## 1. Executive read

**This segment sells authority in four separable pieces and nobody in it holds all four.** A member can write the standard, run the laboratory that tests to it, issue the mark that says a product passed, and sign the report a lender relies on. DNV holds all four. UL Solutions and Intertek hold two each and different second ones. Sargent & Lundy holds one. CSA Group holds two today and one after its sale closes. **Five members, four distinct combinations** — and the two that share a combination differ instead on who engages them.

**The party with the largest share of the North American listing business holds the fewest pieces, and the gap is disclosed to the dollar.** UL Solutions owns the UL Mark but not UL 9540, UL 9540A or UL 1973 — UL Standards & Engagement publishes them, holds about 61.6 per cent of the equity and 94.1 per cent of the voting power, and is paid **USD 22m in 2025** for standards-library access, recorded in cost of revenue, while the mark is licensed to the non-profits royalty-free in the other direction. **A segment whose product is authority, in which the party issuing most of its market's listings licenses that authority from somebody else, is the finding.**

**And no league table covers the roster, which is why the coverage boundary is the opening section.** Four instruments touch it, the largest covers four of seven, and the two that rank on revenue do not share a company. §3 sets that out as a measurement.

**Three changes of control are pending and all three target the fourth quarter of 2026 — the brief named two.** CSA Group is selling its laboratories and keeping its standards; Intertek is being taken private on a court-sanctioned scheme; and UL Solutions is buying an independent NRTL and its mark. **Two change a role label on this roster; the third changes the map it competes on.** None of the three carries a day-level date anywhere in the record.
