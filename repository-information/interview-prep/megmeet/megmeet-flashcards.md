# Megmeet — Flashcards (cram deck)

The 14 core company cards are in the app: Profiler → Megmeet → **Study guide 📖** (tap-to-flip). The cards below are the interview-specific extras. Private prep material (not deployed).

**Q: "Why do you want to work at Megmeet?" (have YOUR answer, but the company hook is:)**
A: A platform power-electronics company at the exact center of the AI infrastructure build-out — the only mainland power vendor NVIDIA named for GB200, moving first on the 800VDC transition — while still diversified across appliances, EV, and industrial. Engineering-led (12% R&D for a decade), going global (Thailand, Dallas, HK listing).

**Q: What does "800VDC transition" mean and why does it matter?**
A: AI racks are hitting power densities (100 kW → 1 MW+) where legacy in-rack AC power wastes space, copper, and efficiency. Moving conversion to a sidecar cabinet distributing 800VDC cuts copper ~45% and lifts end-to-end efficiency ~5% (Megmeet's claims). NVIDIA is driving this for Kyber/Rubin-era racks from ~2027 — whoever co-designs now wins the sockets.

**Q: Name the AI power chain, top to bottom.**
A: SST (grid → 800VDC, >98.5%) → 800V Sidecar (>1 MW/rack) → power shelves (33 kW air / 110 kW liquid-cooled) → BBU + supercap backup shelves → CRPS/M-CRPS PSUs (to 5.5 kW Ruby) → Power Brick at the GPU.

**Q: Who are their competitors in server power?**
A: Delta (~41%), Lite-On, AcBel, Chicony, Advanced Energy — Taiwanese vendors ~74% combined. Reports say Megmeet is displacing Lite-On as #2 on NVIDIA power shelves (unconfirmed).

**Q: What was FY2025, in one honest sentence?**
A: Revenue ¥9.4B up 15%, but profit fell two-thirds to ¥146M because they spent through the P&L — AI-power R&D, overseas plants, price wars, FX — to buy position in the AI transition.

**Q: What's the nearest catalyst?**
A: H1 2026 results, ~August 27 — first hard read on the AI-DC power ramp.

**Q: Their welding business — why mention it?**
A: Shows you know they're not a one-story company: digital inverter welders (Artsen/Dex), 1,800 robot-welding power sources repeat order from Honglu Steel, 38.5% gross margin — the margin engine while AI ramps.

**Q: The founder's lineage?**
A: Tong Yongsheng — PhD NUAA, Zhejiang post-doc; Shenzhen Huawei Electric VP (1996–2001), Emerson Network Power VP (2001–2005), founded Megmeet 2003/2005. Most of the exec bench shares the Huawei Electric → Emerson power lineage.
