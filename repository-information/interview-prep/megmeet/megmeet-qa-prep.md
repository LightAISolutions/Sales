## Questions they may ask you

**"What do you know about Megmeet?"** — Your moment. Deliver the one-paragraph story (study-guide §1), then ONE level deeper on whichever leg fits the role you're interviewing for. Land one number (¥9.4B revenue, +15%) and one specific (NVIDIA 800V HVDC ecosystem / GB200 power vendor). 60–90 seconds, then stop.

**"Why Megmeet / why this role?"** — Tie your background to their direction: a diversified power-electronics platform putting everything behind the AI data-center transition, going global (Thailand, Dallas lab, HK listing). Genuine specificity beats flattery — mention something concrete like the 110 kW liquid-cooled shelf for GB300-class racks or the sidecar architecture.

**"What do you think of our market position?"** — The mature frame: "You're attacking a Delta-dominated market not head-on but through the 800VDC architecture change, with NVIDIA co-design as the wedge — classic timing play. The proof point everyone's watching is the H1 report and whether AI revenue ramps in 2026." Shows you understand both the opportunity and that it's not yet won.

**"Any concerns about the company?" (or you sense they're testing realism)** — Sympathetic honesty: "FY2025 profits absorbed the cost of the pivot — R&D, overseas build-outs, FX. That's what a deliberate bet looks like; the question is execution on the 2026 ramp." Never recite the bear case unprompted; never pretend there isn't one.

**"What do you know about our products?" (technical roles)** — Walk the grid-to-GPU chain from memory: SST → sidecar → shelves → BBU/supercap → M-CRPS → Power Brick. If welding/industrial/appliance role: Artsen digital welders / MV-series drives / inverter appliance controllers respectively — know YOUR segment's flagships deepest.

## Questions to ask them (pick ~3; tailor to interviewer)

1. **The ramp:** "Megmeet moved earliest among mainland vendors on the 800VDC sidecar — how is the team balancing that architecture bet against the nearer-term M-CRPS/power-shelf volume business?"
2. **The role's context:** "How does this role connect to the AI data-center push — is the growth mostly overseas (Dallas/Thailand) or domestic hyperscalers?"
3. **Organization:** "The High-Efficiency Power BU merged storage/charging with network energy — how integrated are the AI-DC and energy-storage power teams day to day?"
4. **(Sales-flavored roles)** "The customer list runs from Ericsson and Cisco to Geely and Haier — how does the sales org divide platform accounts vs the new AI-infrastructure accounts?"
5. **(Culture)** "Much of the leadership shares the Huawei Electric–Emerson lineage — how would you describe what that engineering culture feels like inside?"

Ask #1 or #2 early if there's room — they signal you understand the strategic moment, not just the Wikipedia version.

## Things NOT to do

- Don't claim they're "NVIDIA's #2 power supplier" as fact — that's trade-press speculation; the company itself called 2025 AI orders immaterial. Say "positioned in the NVIDIA 800V ecosystem."
- Don't bring up the July stock crash, guarantees, or dilution unprompted. If asked about the stock, pivot to fundamentals and the Aug 27 H1 report.
- Don't over-index on AI if the role is in another segment — leading with THEIR segment shows better homework than reciting the hot story.
