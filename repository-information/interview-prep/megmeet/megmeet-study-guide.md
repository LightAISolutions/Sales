## 1. The company in one paragraph (memorize this shape, use your own words)

Megmeet is a Shenzhen power-electronics platform company (founded 2003 by Tong Yongsheng, a Huawei Electric → Emerson Network Power veteran; SZSE: 002851). It applies one core competence — power conversion and control — across six businesses: appliance electric controls (biggest, ~38% of revenue), power products (~28.5%, includes the AI data-center power line), EV & rail components (~12%, fastest growing), industrial automation (~9%), welding/intelligent equipment (~6%, best margins), and precision connection (~5%). FY2025 revenue ¥9.4B (+15%), ~40% overseas, ~8,900 employees, R&D ~12% of revenue for a decade. The strategic bet: becoming NVIDIA's mainland-China power partner for the 800VDC AI data-center transition.

## 2. The AI data-center story (their #1 talking point — know it cold)

- **The position:** only mainland-China power supplier NVIDIA named for GB200 NVL72 (Oct 2024); member of NVIDIA's 800V HVDC alliance (May 2025) alongside Delta, Flex, Lite-On.
- **The product chain (grid → GPU):** solid-state transformer (>98.5% efficiency, outputs 800VDC) → 800V HVDC Sidecar cabinet (>1 MW per rack, for Kyber-class racks; claims +5% end-to-end efficiency, −45% copper) → power shelves (33 kW air-cooled; 110 kW liquid-cooled for GB300/Vera Rubin) → BBU (16.5 kW) & supercapacitor (15 kW) backup shelves → CRPS/M-CRPS server PSUs (800 W–5.5 kW, Titanium/Ruby efficiency, >100 W/in³) → Power Brick point-of-load.
- **The honest caveat (do NOT overclaim in the room):** the company itself said (Nov 2025) AI-server orders were small-batch and immaterial through 2025. Batch deliveries started end-2025; domestic hyperscaler volume expected 2026. If you cite their NVIDIA position, frame it as "positioned for the ramp," not "already dominating."
- **The competitive frame:** server PSUs are ~74% Taiwanese (Delta ~41%). Megmeet's wedge is the architecture transition — AC in-rack power → 800VDC sidecar/SST — where first-mover co-design with NVIDIA can reshuffle share. 1MW-rack adoption is projected from ~2027 (Rubin era).
- **US-facing moves:** Dallas TX power test lab (Jun 2026, 360 kW → 1.5 MW roadmap), Thailand plant phase II, HK listing filed — a supply chain packaged for US hyperscaler procurement.

## 4. Six segments — one line each (breadth signal)

1. **Appliance controls** — inverter HVAC/heat-pump/washer controllers + smart-bathroom (Yihe); Xiaomi/Haier/Hisense; declining −5% in price wars.
2. **Power products** — data-center/AI power + network power (Ericsson, Cisco, Juniper, Arista) + medical/industrial PSUs.
3. **EV & rail** — motor controllers, OBC/DC-DC, thermal, active suspension; Geely/BAIC/Leapmotor; +109% but 15% GM.
4. **Industrial automation** — MV800/MV520 drives, M5 servo, MU PLCs; +40%.
5. **Intelligent equipment** — Artsen/Dex digital welders, robotic welding power (Honglu 1,800-unit repeat order), laser welding, industrial microwave, oil ESPCP; 38.5% GM.
6. **Precision connection** — magnet wire/FFC/FPC feeding EV packs and storage; investment phase.
