# esVolta — Technology Lesson Plan

*Full-depth curriculum behind the in-app study guide (`live-site-pages/profiler-data/esvolta.study.json`). Not deployed. Baseline: high-school STEM. Dossier: `esvolta.profile.json` (schema v7, v1, 2026-09-05).*

## What this plan teaches, and what it leaves to other guides

esVolta is the corpus's first **mixed contracted-and-merchant** storage owner: resource-adequacy batteries in California, hedged-plus-merchant batteries in Texas, and about USD 1.5 billion of disclosed facilities layered on the mix — with the sponsor now running a sale process. The plan teaches (1) the three ways a battery is paid and what each leaves the owner, (2) why the allocation is made market by market, (3) how a lender's DSCR model treats contracted, hedged and merchant revenue, (4) the capital stack a mixed book needs (pre-NTP corporate facility, project debt, preferred equity, tax equity and credit transfers), and (5) what a sponsor's sale process does to a mixed book.

It does **not** re-teach: the tolling revenue model, ancillary stacking or duration arithmetic (Classroom); the fully contracted platform and the sponsor exit (Aypa guide); the fully merchant developer and the lender that lends against a desk (Spearmint guide); RA vs toll vs revenue swap from the buyer's chair (GridStor guide); the originator that sells the contract (Strata guide).

## Module 1 — Three ways a battery is paid (45 min)

- **Contracted (California).** Resource adequacy: every load-serving entity must show contracted capacity for its peak plus a reserve; a battery is paid a fixed monthly amount per MW for availability and keeps dispatch. esVolta: Hummingbird (PG&E RA), Santa Paula (a commodity firm plus CCAs), Black Walnut (CalChoice), Wildcat and Acorn (SCE 2017 IDER RFO).
- **Hedged (Texas).** ERCOT is energy-only — no capacity payment — so a floor is bought from a trader: a revenue put, a swap or a fixed-for-floating contract. esVolta's Texas trio: 'a hedge in place with a confidential commodity market participant' plus wholesale participation (CEO to Utility Dive, January 2025). Counterparty, tenor and volume undisclosed — normal for a private bilateral contract.
- **Merchant.** Five-minute settlement-point prices plus ancillary clearing prices. ERCOT battery revenues: about USD 193/kW (2023) → USD 56/kW (2024) → USD 29/kW (2025) per Modo.
- **Corporate offtake (Boxcar).** 'A long-term offtake agreement ... with a major corporate customer' — a contract in a market with none by default; unnamed.
- **Check:** why is Texas hedged rather than contracted? (No RA market; a floor must be bought.)

## Module 2 — The book by market (30 min)

Walk the dossier's project ledger: Anole 240/480, Desert Willow 150/300, Burksol 100/200 (ERCOT, operating 2025, Sungrow PowerTitan, Saber Power and Quanta as EPCs); Hummingbird 75/300 (CAISO, company says March 2026); Santa Paula 30/60; Black Walnut 15/60; Boxcar 150/300 and City Breeze 140/280 under construction; Cactus Wren 800 MW / 3,200 MWh permitted in Pinal County (SRP 'in active discussions'); Gila Monster 50 MW (PNM); Tava Mountain 100 MW; Marmot Hills, Holly Oaks; Beartooth stalled. Markets per the company: CAISO, ERCOT, WECC, expanding into SPP and MISO — not PJM.

## Module 3 — What a lender does with the mix (50 min)

- **DSCR.** Cash available ÷ debt service, kept above a floor in a downside case. Contracted revenue at face value for the term; hedged at the floor for the tenor; merchant at a haircut forecast, often only after the hedge ends. Same battery, less debt at each step.
- **Structure follows the mix.** Texas trio: USD 258 million across five lenders (MUFG, Nomura, KeyBanc as co-leads; Investec; Cadence) — construction, tax-equity bridge, LCs, term. Boxcar with a contract: MUFG alone, USD 139.6 million.
- **Preferred equity.** Captona's USD 243 million 'including ITC transfer proceeds' (January 2025) sits between the banks and the owner; fixed return ahead of common; absorbs merchant risk banks will not lend against. Not a sale — esVolta stayed owner-operator.
- **The credits.** ITC earned at placement in service regardless of revenue model: tax equity (U.S. Bancorp at Santa Paula, Greenprint USD 110 million at Hummingbird), transfer inside preferred (Captona), plain transfer (Computacenter at Black Walnut). Tax-equity bridge loans advance the money.
- **Corporate top.** Nomura-led pre-NTP facility: USD 185 million (April 2024) → up to USD 450 million (June 2026), CIP Green Credit Funds the largest lender; funds development, long-lead equipment, pre-construction. Underwrites the platform — book, pipeline, sponsor.

## Module 4 — When the sponsor sells (35 min)

- Generate Capital acquired esVolta July 2022 (price undisclosed). New Project Media (January 2026): 'exploring strategic alternatives', tied to Generate's own capital constraints; CEO Mann: 'not currently looking to sell any material assets'. Infralogic (5 June 2026): Barclays and Truist mandated; Generate's CEO: 'good interest'. The company's June 2026 release still says 'portfolio company of Generate Capital'.
- A mixed book is priced in two parts (contracted/hedged at a low discount rate; merchant and pipeline at a high one); the bidder's identity — fund, trading house, IPP — sets the weighting.
- The lenders' signal: the corporate facility was upsized during the process.
- Cross-reference the Aypa guide's sponsor-exit chapter; the operating company is the object, not the cause.

## Module 5 — Where it fails (25 min)

The hedge may be thinner than the word; a buyer that wants only the contracted half; one integrator, one country (Sungrow on all three Texas sites, design basis at Boxcar; no FEOC statement); California contracts arrive late (Hummingbird selected 2018, operating 2026 per the company, unconfirmed); the pipeline outruns the book (Cactus Wren six times the largest project, no contract).

## Module 6 — The industry map (20 min)

Aypa (contracted, sponsor sale signed), Spearmint (merchant, lender against the desk), GridStor (contracted, same Modo 'pure-play' group), Strata (tolls sold; same lead bank, Nomura), Hunt Energy Network (fully merchant, own QSE), Intersect (energy parks, Google). CIP's credit funds lend to esVolta while CIP's equity fund bought Strata's Scatter Wash — one manager on both sides of the Arizona storage market.

## Corrections to the brief

- Present in CAISO and ERCOT; **not PJM** (company market list; no PJM project in any source since 2017).
- The Captona transaction was preferred equity plus ITC transfer proceeds, not a project sale.
- Generate acquired esVolta in July 2022, not 2023.
- Sponsor sale process: Barclays and Truist mandated (Infralogic, June 2026); no sponsor release, valuation or timeline.

## Suggested pacing

Two sittings: Modules 1–3 (about 2 h 5 min), then Modules 4–6 (about 1 h 20 min) after reading the Aypa guide's sponsor-exit section. The quiz's DSCR and Captona questions are the two to get right.
