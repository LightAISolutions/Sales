# Macquarie — Technology Lesson Plan

**Subject:** Macquarie Group Limited (ASX: MQG) · **Written:** 2026-09-06 · **Baseline assumed:** high-school STEM, no finance background.

**What this teaches.** The complete life cycle of an infrastructure investment, using the only position in this corpus that has one — Aligned Data Centers, bought at 85 MW in 2018 and sold at more than 6 GW in 2026. Then the reading skills the case demands: enterprise value against equity, build-out against multiple arbitrage, and financier against owner.

**Why this gap.** Every other investor guide catches its subject mid-hold. This is the only one that can teach an exit, and the exit is where the fund lifecycle becomes visible. It is also the corpus's clearest case of a firm systematically misread as an owner when it is lending.

## Module 1 — The fund lifecycle
Raise, deploy, build, harvest. Why a closed-end fund must sell however well the asset is performing, and how that contractual clock differs from a perpetual vehicle's absence of one. The Aligned timeline as the spine.

## Module 2 — Two ways to make money
Platform build-out against multiple arbitrage. Why a seventy-fold capacity increase can only be the first. What the buyer inherits in each case. Exercise: given entry and exit capacity and price, argue which mechanism dominated and say what evidence would change your mind.

## Module 3 — Enterprise value is not the cheque
Enterprise value, equity consideration, and the debt between them. The Aligned figures: about USD 40bn against about USD 21bn, with no published debt figure reconciling them. Then growth capital committed at closing, which is not part of the price at all. Exercise: given an enterprise value and an equity figure, infer the implied net debt and state what would falsify the inference.

## Module 4 — Financier or owner
The four operating groups and what each one does with money — fund equity, balance-sheet principal investment, and lending. The corpus table: Aligned and Applied Digital are equity; Fluidstack and Fermi America are credit; Trina Storage is tax equity on one project. Why the operating group named in a source is the reliable tell.

## Module 5 — Researching a company that files somewhere else
Macquarie is not an SEC periodic-reporting registrant, and a researcher who stops there has made a routing error. The correct method: find the real route (the company's own asset paths, the exchange announcement service), then **bound the null with a control** — searching the same name unrestricted returns over ten thousand filings by other companies. The transferable rule: a null on a name bounds only that name.

Also in this module: the four traps — the 31 March fiscal year, the AUD/USD split, the MAM restatement from A$1,610m to A$2,049m, and the deregistered Macquarie Infrastructure Corporation look-alike.

## Pacing
Five modules, roughly 45 minutes each. Modules 1-3 form a sequence and should be taken in order. Module 5 is a research-method module and is useful well beyond this company.
