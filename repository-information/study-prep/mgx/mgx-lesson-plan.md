# MGX — Technology Lesson Plan

**Purpose:** teach **the capital layer above the landlords, labs and power suppliers this corpus covers** — what a sovereign wealth fund is and why the Gulf has so many; how a fund, a platform, a joint venture and a direct stake differ as vehicles and what each discloses; how to size an investor that publishes no ticket sizes (round roles, who quotes whom, regulatory registers); why frontier labs take sovereign money and what they withhold; the circular-financing pattern when one investor funds the tenant, the landlord and the builder; and why export controls and CFIUS — not returns — are the binding constraints on where sovereign AI capital can buy compute. MGX is the worked example because two years after its creation it is an initial equity funder of Stargate, a co-founder of the AI Infrastructure Partnership that owns Aligned Data Centers, an owner in Vantage, and a co-lead investor in OpenAI and Anthropic — the names that anchor a dozen dossiers here — while publishing almost no numbers.

**How this plan relates to what you already have.** The **Anthropic** and **OpenAI** plans teach the compute buyer's contracts and the offtake as project finance; the **Aligned** and **Vantage** dossiers carry the landlord's economics; the **Excelsior**, **GridStor** and **Aypa** plans teach fund managers that own power plants. This plan is about the investor who sits above all of them and repeats none of that material.

**Three ideas carry the plan.** A sovereign fund is the most patient capital in the market and the most scrutinised. When an investor publishes no numbers, its roles are its size. And for AI capital the licence regime is the supply chain.

**One boundary, held throughout.** MGX publishes no annual report, AUM total, stakes, tickets or headcount. Every figure in this plan is either MGX's own (the USD 49 billion Fund I close, 15% of TikTok USDS, USD 2 billion for Binance), a counterparty's (round sizes, the Aligned enterprise value) or press-reported and labelled so (Bloomberg's about USD 7 billion Stargate figure). mgx.ae pages are ~15 MB each and were read with curl; WAM, Business Wire and openai.com were unreadable and identical texts on mgx.ae or SoftBank's site were used.

**Suggested pacing:** Module 1 (~25 min). Module 2 with MGX's portfolio page open (~35 min). Module 3 (~30 min). Module 4 (~30 min). Module 5 with the Warren statements open (~30 min). Then flashcards and self-test in the app.

## Module 1 — What a sovereign wealth fund is, and why the Gulf has so many

**The single idea:** a state-owned investment pool that turns a depleting or volatile income (oil, reserves) into a permanent, diversified one, answering to a ruler or council rather than to outside investors.

**Abu Dhabi's family of funds.** ADIA (reserve fund); Mubadala (about USD 330 billion, the strategic developer that built GlobalFoundries); ADQ (domestic infrastructure); G42 (the state's AI operating group). MGX: created 12 March 2024 by the Artificial Intelligence and Advanced Technology Council with Mubadala and G42 as "foundational partners", chaired by the council's chairman Sheikh Tahnoon bin Zayed (also UAE National Security Advisor and G42's chairman), run by Ahmed Yahia Al Idrissi (ex-CEO of Mubadala's direct investments). Stated purpose: "a UAE national champion focused on AI and advanced technologies".

**Why structure explains behaviour.** A national champion can commit USD 49 billion to one theme, take stakes with no published price, and be welcomed into rounds a private fund could not enter — because its counterparties are dealing with a state. The same fact is why US politicians ask who sits behind it.

**Self-check:** what does MGX Fund I's USD 49 billion close from "institutional and private investors from the Gulf, North America, Asia and Europe" tell you that its creation release did not? *(That third-party capital, not only Mubadala's and G42's, is in the vehicle — MGX manages others' money as well as the state's.)*

## Module 2 — Fund, platform, joint venture, direct stake

**The single idea:** the vehicle decides who controls what, who else is in the room, and what gets disclosed.

**Closed-end fund.** MGX Fund I LP (ADGM, established May 2024) plus an alternative investment vehicle and two co-investment partnerships; USD 49 billion of commitments against a USD 45 billion target; 14 companies; LPs unnamed. MGX Fund Management Limited is the licensed manager (FSRA, 18 October 2024).

**Platform.** The AI Infrastructure Partnership (launched September 2024 as GAIIP) co-founded with BlackRock, GIP and Microsoft: USD 30 billion equity target, USD 100 billion with debt; NVIDIA, xAI and Cisco as technology partners; Kuwait Investment Authority and Temasek as anchors; GE Vernova and NextEra Energy as energy collaborators. MGX's CEO is AIP's Vice Chairman, its CLO sits on AIP's investment committee, David Scott on its board. First investment: Aligned Data Centers.

**Joint venture.** Stargate LLC — SoftBank's release names "SoftBank, OpenAI, Oracle, and MGX" as initial equity funders of a USD 500 billion programme; MGX's share undisclosed; later Stargate releases omit MGX; The Information reported the JV "hasn't hired any staff" a year on. Campus AI — Bpifrance, Mistral, NVIDIA, MGX; a 1.4 GW Paris-region campus (construction H2 2026, operations 2028) expanded toward 3 GW in June 2026.

**Direct stakes.** OpenAI (five follow-ons; co-lead of the USD 122 billion round), Anthropic (Series G co-lead; Series H significant investor), xAI (Series C, USD 20 billion Series E; merged into SpaceX), Databricks (four rounds), Isomorphic Labs; Binance USD 2 billion minority; TikTok USDS JV 15%; Altera 51% with Silver Lake (Intel keeps 49%); Khazna minority with Silver Lake; Vantage "to support the Stargate build-out of 2.3 gigawatts".

**Direct and platform at once.** Aligned's closing release names "AIP, MGX, and BlackRock's GIP" as buyers — MGX inside AIP and beside it. Co-investment lets a large deal be done without breaching the platform's concentration limits and gives the co-investor a larger slice.

**Exercise:** classify each of the 13 names on MGX's portfolio page into the four vehicle types; note which have a stated percentage (two) and which have a stated dollar amount (one).

## Module 3 — Reading a fund that publishes no numbers

**The single idea:** when tickets are undisclosed, roles are the size signal.

**The grammar of a round.** Lead (sets price and terms, largest slice) → co-lead → participant (takes an allocation on the lead's terms) → anchor (first large commitment whose name brings others). A follow-on keeps or grows a stake in a later round; a secondary buys existing shares (the company gets no new money).

**MGX's OpenAI sequence read in these terms.** Participant (October 2024) → "additional major investment" in the USD 300 billion round (2025) → employee secondary (early 2026) → co-lead of the USD 122 billion round (March 2026), "MGX's fifth follow-on". Anthropic: participant by May 2025 → co-lead February 2026 → significant investor May 2026. Databricks: new investor December 2024 → co-lead September 2025. Escalation inside two years is the size signal.

**Who quotes whom.** MGX's CEO is a named speaker in BlackRock's, Microsoft's and GIP's AIP releases and in Aligned's; David Scott is Aligned's media contact. Stargate UAE's releases (G42, OpenAI, Oracle, NVIDIA, SoftBank, Cisco) never name MGX — its role there runs through Khazna, a landlord it part-owns.

**The register.** The ADGM FSRA file: licence 18 October 2024, permissions widened June 2025, four funds, twelve approved persons, two licensed directors withdrawn and two added in May 2026 with no MGX statement. A regulator's register is the one document a private fund cannot decline to file.

**Self-check:** the mgx.ae news index reads "$18B Series K" for Databricks; MGX's X post and board release say USD 1 billion. Which do you record, and how? *(USD 1 billion, corroborated twice; note the index figure as a probable site typo.)*

## Module 4 — Why labs take Gulf money, and the circle it closes

**The single idea (labs):** compute bills of tens of billions a year can be funded by few investors; sovereign funds are among them, and the lab's protection is to withhold governance.

**The evidence.** Amodei's 2025 memo via WIRED: Gulf capital is "easily $100B or more"; "We gain a very large benefit from having access to this capital"; "This is a real downside and I'm not thrilled about it"; deals to be "narrowly scoped, purely financial". MGX then co-led Anthropic's Series G and joined Series H with no board seat.

**The single idea (circle):** the same investor funds the tenant, the landlord and the builder.

**Trace it on the corpus map.** OpenAI (tenant) ← Stargate LLC (MGX co-funder) ← Vantage builds Stargate's Texas and Wisconsin campuses (MGX investor) ← Oracle operates (MGX co-managing investor in TikTok USDS). Aligned (landlord) ← AIP (MGX co-founder) with NVIDIA and xAI as partners; MGX backs xAI and Anthropic, who lease from such landlords; NVIDIA sells chips into all of it and sits inside AIP and Campus AI with MGX. The Anthropic dossier quotes NVIDIA's CFO on the objection ("we know some will call this circular financing. We see it differently").

**Two limits.** MGX's percentages are undisclosed at every link, so the circle's thickness cannot be measured. And MGX owns no power: its energy exposure runs entirely through AIP's GE Vernova and NextEra agreements ("all forms of energy"), Aligned's cooling and a 31 MW / 62 MWh battery, and Campus AI's EDF and RTE partnership.

## Module 5 — Export controls and CFIUS: the constraints that bind

**The single idea:** a sovereign fund buying AI compute faces two federal regimes that a sovereign fund buying farmland does not.

**Export controls.** They decide where advanced chips and servers may be shipped and who may run them. Background: Biden-era proposed limits on the UAE; G42's prior Commerce scrutiny over China ties; House and Senate Democrats (May 2025) opposing "very large volumes of advanced AI chips to the UAE". July 2026: Commerce moves the UAE to Country Group A:5 (licence-free shipment of certain sensitive technology) and, per CNBC, "said it would view favorably export applications for semiconductors and server equipment destined for MGX". Senator Warren's 10 July statement names MGX and calls for testimony; her August letter links it to a reported USD 500 million UAE-linked stake in World Liberty Financial. For an investor 70% allocated to North America whose UAE campuses need US chips, the licence regime is the supply chain — and it can be reversed.

**CFIUS.** Reviews foreign acquisitions of US businesses for national-security risk. MGX's 15% of TikTok USDS Joint Venture LLC exists because of a divestiture law and a national-security process: Oracle as "Trusted Security Partner", MGX's David Scott on the security committee. Any future MGX acquisition of a US landlord or lab stake above the passive threshold runs the same review.

**Political risk.** The USD 2 billion Binance investment settled in World Liberty Financial's USD1 stablecoin (May 2025) drew Senate letters to MGX and Binance and resurfaced in the 2026 export-control debate. Not a finding against MGX — but the reason counsel now asks who the investor is before the term sheet.

**Where it fails** (recap for the app's callout): the JV that never forms; access without control; the licence as supply chain; reputational contagion; opacity that hides risk from everyone including the investor's own LPs; concentration in a flagship a national champion cannot easily sell.

**Self-check:** which single US administrative act in 2026 most changed MGX's ability to equip its UAE campuses, and who contested it within weeks? *(Commerce's Country Group A:5 decision and its stated favourable review for MGX; Senator Warren.)*
